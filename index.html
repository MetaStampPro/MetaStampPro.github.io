06Questions, answered

The details
matter.

Does MetaStamp upload my files?

No. Media processing and review happen on the device. An iCloud original that has not downloaded yet may still need a network connection from Apple to fetch the source.

Does it edit the original?

Never by default. MetaStamp writes a new copy into a private temporary workspace, then gives you a choice of where to save or share it.

Can a watermark stop someone from removing it?

No. A watermark makes the intended use visible. It is a practical boundary for a handoff, not a promise that screenshots or editing are impossible.

Which formats are supported?

The current workflow is focused on image files. Video, Live Photo, PDF, and Office paths have format-specific boundaries and should only be marked ready when MetaStamp can verify the exported copy.
